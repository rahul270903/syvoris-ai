# SYVORIS Ai™ — Competitor Feature Coverage Matrix
**Benchmarked Against Zoho CRM, Creatio CRM, and Vtiger CRM**  
*Document Version: 1.0.0 (Phase 0 Discovery Baseline)*  
*Status: Approved Blueprint*

---

## 1. Executive Benchmarking Methodology

This matrix compares the planned and prototype capabilities of **SYVORIS Ai™** against three leading enterprise and mid-market CRM platforms:
1. **Zoho CRM (v2024 / Enterprise & Ultimate Tiers):** Renowned for comprehensive sales automation, omnichannel communication, Zia AI, and deep ecosystem breadth.
2. **Creatio CRM (Freedom UI / Studio, Sales, Marketing, Service):** Renowned for composable no-code architecture, BPMN 2.0 process orchestration, and dynamic case management.
3. **Vtiger CRM (V9 Cloud / Calculus AI):** Renowned for unified inventory-to-cash capabilities, built-in help desk SLAs, and sales enablement.

### Standard Status Classifications
- **Competitor Availability:** `Available`, `Partially Available`, `Not Available`, `Requires Add-on`, `Requires Integration`, `Not Verified`.
- **SYVORIS Prototype Status:** `Fully Implemented`, `Partially Implemented`, `UI Only`, `Simulated`, `Missing`, `Requires External Integration`.
- **Implementation Priority:**
  - `P0`: Essential platform foundation and core CRM functionality.
  - `P1`: Essential business operations, CPQ, and workflow automation.
  - `P2`: Advanced AI agents, analytics, customization, and external connectors.
  - `P3`: Advanced enterprise ecosystem, multi-region compliance, and deep partner integrations.
